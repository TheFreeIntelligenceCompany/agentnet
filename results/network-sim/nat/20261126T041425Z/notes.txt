1.r_view	R announces extip 172.30.80.10; the clients run --nat=none so they announce no address of their own. What R 'believes' about a client is the observed remote address of the TCP connection, i.e. the gateway's public address plus the translated source port. Observed mapping: tcp 6 431998 ESTABLISHED src=192.168.101.10 dst=172.30.80.10 sport=44530 dport=60000 src=172.30.80.10 dst=172.30.80.21 sport=60000 dport=44530 [ASSURED] mark=0 use=1 (full conntrack in evidence/conntrack-initial.txt).
4.idle_with_daemons	The daemons are not silent while 'idle': each runs a Store history sync every history_sync_interval_s (default 15 s), a Store query to R over the existing libp2p connection, so the connection carries packets at least that often and the mapping never ages out. This phase therefore does NOT show whether libp2p itself keeps a mapping alive; the node-only phase does.
